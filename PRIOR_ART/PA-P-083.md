## Primary-source reading and comparison

- **Zhang, Yeddanapudi & Mosterman (2008), IFAC** — original proceedings PDF search excerpt and publisher abstract reviewed; event detection/localization must address missed zero crossings, repeated triggering and chattering. This is the closest **same-purpose published predecessor**; predictor/root finding is not an algorithmic novelty claim here. The hosted proceedings PDF and abstract may differ on DOI suffix: linked both without guessing.
- **Guglielmi & Hairer (2022), Numerical Algorithms** — **open-access full paper, introduction and event-location section read**, DOI 10.1007/s11075-021-01154-1. Existing event-driven methods localize discontinuity crossings; their contribution includes handling sliding modes/codimension-2 intersections, not a speed claim for this model. Their §3 discusses event location as a standard step. 
- **Kong et al. (2024), Proceedings of the IEEE** — IEEE primary abstract reviewed, DOI 10.1109/JPROC.2024.3440211. Saltation accounts for event-time sensitivity, not a new event-time inverse operator.
- **SciPy** — actual source/doc read: `solve_ivp` uses bracketing Brent root location with dense solution; valuable correctness comparator, but a Python-level IVP is a **weak speed baseline** against a closed-form affine flow.

**Nearest published method:** general zero-crossing localization for hybrid simulation (Zhang et al. 2008), plus established exact linear affine propagation and safeguarded scalar root finding. **Fair rival:** same closed-form affine flow with JIT compiled Newton/bracket root finding (not only SciPy's Python integrator). All rivals may cache coefficients and share formulas.
**Exact unresolved delta:** No novel numerical method is proposed; the unmeasured question is whether a basic quadratic event-time warm start has useful *workload-specific* end-to-end savings versus tuned exact-flow root finders, under explicit 1e-7 state, 1e-9 time, and zero event-itinerary errors.
**Novelty:** KNOWN_METHOD_BENCHMARK; the above searches are non-exhaustive and do not establish novelty. No industrial/certified/renderer claim.
**Caveats:** Review covers original accessible paper/abstract and library code, not an exhaustive industrial solver benchmark; the planned toy is a timer-reset two-state affine hybrid, not Filippov sliding/grazing/general chaos.
