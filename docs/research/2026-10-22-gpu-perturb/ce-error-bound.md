# CE prototype arithmetic model — conditional research model, NOT portable proof

The one GPU32 CE prototype reuses ff.wgsl CE/FF arithmetic; it does not create another orbit recurrence. Its scalar error radii remain inflated f32+exponent values. The physical CE addition error and upper-norm approximation are separate: 1.0001 norm inflation bounds magnitude, not an additive state perturbation.

With binary32 round-to-nearest unit roundoff u=2^-24, nonoverlapping hi/lo inputs, exact error-free TwoSum/TwoProd transforms and no harmful underflow, omitted lo*lo in FF multiplication is O(u²) of the hi product. Cross-term rounding and residual recombination are also O(u²). Counting four FF products plus two FF sums per complex multiplication, and the two products/two sums of delta'=2Zdelta+delta²+dc, motivates a conservative diagnostic stage allowance256u² times the sum of absolute term magnitudes. The same allowance is charged to derivative update and physical CE addition. A discarded aligned exponent tail is an absolute error relative to the larger operand, not a relative error of a potentially cancelled result. Power-of-two normalization is exact only while intermediate components remain normal; underflow losses must be separately bounded or rejected. This is a proposed RN operation-count model, NOT a completed rigorous derivation for every shader execution.

Crucial limitation found before the first corpus run: WGSL does NOT promise round-to-nearest. Its definition of correctly rounded permits either adjacent representable value; subnormal flushing and reassociation are permitted, and fma may inherit unfused x*y+z error. See primary specification https://www.w3.org/TR/WGSL/#floating-point-accuracy and https://www.w3.org/TR/WGSL/#floating-point-evaluation . Thus an IEEE RN EFT theorem does not establish this prototype's portable error contract. ff_init's runtime reassociation guard and FMA/Dekker self-test, plus actual device primitive tests, are empirical capability evidence; they cannot prove all inputs obey RN or validate256u² universally. No fitted observed-error factor may repair this logical gap.

Admission contract: DEC-10/16 permit measured heuristic approximation contracts; GPU-27/28 provide the existing runtime-self-test and whole-frame precedent. GPU-32 is an explicit experimental Heuristic route under that contract. Its RN estimate is conditional, not a rigorous portable bound. Bound/certified evidence requests are refused, runtime FF checks can decline, all output guards remain active, and whole-frame comparison is required for tested depths. No default/automatic/film or speed promotion is authorized. GPU Float full-reference and original CPU fallback reference are deliberately separate: applying the former to CPU fallback caused two measured nu regressions, while the original fallback preserved the landed baseline.
