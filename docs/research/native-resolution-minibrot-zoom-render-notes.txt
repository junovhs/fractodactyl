Render notes from a native-resolution minibrot zoom (fractodactyl 0.5.0, Linux)
Time noted: 5:29 PM

We tried to render a 30 s, 60 fps zoom at native 3840×1600 with the master-ice preset, starting at width 4 and landing on a minibrot. The bundled place journey_various_places does not land on one. Its centre is about 2e-18 from the nucleus that fd zone finds for a period-2287 minibrot (q=1135, r=2, i.e. M(1135,2)), roughly 3×10^62 view widths at the place's 7.5e-81 width. Frames from that place show a boundary region, not a minibrot. We have not established whether the centre was lost when the place was saved or was never on the nucleus. Using the nucleus centre (re ≈ −0.74499578497689141180, im ≈ 0.11310171741105477952) with a landing width of 3e-31, the minibrot body is clearly visible, and about 15% of samples at that width never escape within 100,000 iterations. fd zone declined to build a zone for this minibrot because its zone kernel only supports the M(24,2) family.

Without a zone, the GPU only does shading (about 0.8 s per frame), so the render is CPU-bound. On deep frames it keeps only 9–13 of 24 threads busy on average. Per-frame render times at native resolution, default ss 2 and 100,000 iterations, were 1 s at width 4, 83 s at 1e-15, and about 140 s at the final frames. Interpolating from seven samples gives roughly 36 hours for the full-quality film. A keyframe run (--keyframes 1) over the last 100 frames took 125 s, which extrapolates to about 1–1.5 hours; --keyframes 2 was not measured. Two RTX 3090s with the same name can't be told apart by name, so we selected the second card with MESA_VK_DEVICE_SELECT=10de:2204 MESA_VK_DEVICE_SELECT_FORCE_DEFAULT_DEVICE=1, and fd has no adapter flag. Two smaller issues: --preset NAME resolves looks/ against the current directory, so it fails outside the bundle folder, and --frames 0..0 is rejected while 0..1 renders one frame. No video has been finished.
