# R-013 · Input-specific interval certificates for space-time omission

**Date:** 2026-10-13 · **Track:** EXPLORATORY · **PREREGISTERED: NO** · **POD: NO** · **Evidence:** REPORTED, with executed cross-implementation checks and a downloadable source/raw-data companion; no external independent reproduction · **Proof scope:** the enclosure argument below and exact directed integer evaluation for the specified rational model, conditional on implementation correctness; not a formally verified program · **Novelty:** UNVERIFIED; established coupling/forgetting ingredients, not a new claim for those principles · **Opportunity:** PROMISING FOR TRANSFER TEST, not industrial validation.

## Research decision and question

The owner explicitly requested escape from repeatedly tuning the restricted P-051 chart. This is a different line: eliminate irrelevant space-time computation for a selected terminal-state query in a bounded driven nonlinear system. It does not attempt to reconstruct an unstable full trajectory. The original [P-051](../PROMPTS/P-051.md) frozen MPFR/performance gate remains UNTESTED. [R-011](R-011.md)'s independent certificate audit remains backlog rather than the default next task.

**Prediction:** a recent, local input pattern can make the requested output insensitive to all omitted initial states and exterior histories, even where a global row-sum contraction condition fails. An interval certificate may then cost O(KR), depending on the admitted history K and spatial radius R, rather than O(ND) for N steps of D state variables.

**Falsifiers:** a certified enclosure misses a separately computed fixed-initial-condition answer; signed coupling or discarded boundaries invalidate inclusion; memory-preserving cases are falsely admitted; or a stronger short-memory comparator eliminates the supposed new advantage. No universality or novelty is presumed.

## Prior art as building material, not a rediscovery claim

1. **Propp and Wilson (1996), Exact Sampling with Coupled Markov Chains and Applications to Statistical Mechanics**, especially sections 2.1–2.2. The paper already explains why coalescence can make million- and billion-step histories require the same work, and why bounding extremal histories can avoid enumerating states. [Paper](https://mathexp2018.metelu.net/books/ProppWilson-1996-ExactSamplingWithCoupledMarkovChainsAndApplicationsToStatisticalMechanics.pdf). The finite-state sampling/distributional theorem is not transferred unchanged to the present continuous deterministic IVP.
2. **Manjunath and Jaeger (2013), Echo State Property Linked to an Input**, definitions 2.3–2.4 and lemma 2.2. Pullback images and input-dependent forgetting, including temporarily expanding maps, are already established. [Author manuscript](https://www.ai.rug.nl/minds/uploads/ESP_NC.pdf).
3. **Huber (2004), Perfect Sampling Using Bounding Chains**, establishes bounding-chain methodology beyond straightforward monotonicity. [Author abstract](https://www1.cmc.edu/pages/faculty/MHuber/Research/papers/abstract_huber2004b.html), [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0405284). Abstract inspected, not the full paper.
4. **Pethel, Corron and Bollt (2007), Deconstructing Spatiotemporal Chaos Using Local Symbolic Dynamics**, reports rigorous interval-based local models of coupled map lattices. [Publisher abstract](https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.99.214101). Abstract inspected; its symbolic-dynamics objective is distinct, but local interval truncation must not be called an unprecedented idea.

**Specific construction tested here:** deterministic finite-horizon, selected-component queries; signed local interactions; unknown exterior states replaced by their full invariant range at every time step; directed rational arithmetic; adaptive joint time/space truncation; explicit abstention. This is a candidate transferable assembly of known ingredients, not an established original algorithm. Most of the gain over full replay is expected from known forgetting. A temporal-only comparator below measures that directly.

## Exact model, inputs and task

A ring of D variables has x(0,i)=0 and indices modulo D. Define

    x(t+1,i) = f(s(t,i)),       f(s) = s/(1+|s|),
    s(t,i) = [a0*x(t,i) + al*x(t,i-1) + ar*x(t,i+1) + b(t,i)]/16.

For the sweep, G is in {12,20,24,32,48,64}, a0=G/2, al=G/4, and ar is either +G/4 or -G/4. Thus the global infinity-norm Lipschitz bound is G/16 in {0.75,1.25,1.5,2,3,4}. For positive coupling with G>16, the derivative at the zero-input origin really has an expanding constant-state mode; lack of a global contraction guarantee is not merely a numerical artifact.

The prescribed input b(t,i) is an integer in [-A,A], where A is in {2,8,16,32,64}; its physical amplitude is A/16. It is generated on demand from (t,i,seed), not by simulating a random-number generator prefix. The input schedule is exact and fixed, not an approximate stochastic oracle. Seeds in the main sweep are 1,...,5. No statistical representativeness claim is made for this synthetic hash-generated family.

For reconstruction, all hash operations below are unsigned modulo 2^64: start z=seed XOR ((t+1)*0x9e3779b97f4a7c15) XOR ((i+1)*0xd1b54a32d192ed03); set z=(z XOR (z>>30))*0xbf58476d1ce4e5b9; then z=(z XOR (z>>27))*0x94d049bb133111eb; then z=z XOR (z>>31). Set b=z mod (2A+1)-A.

**Requested numerical output:** one component x(N,center), returned as a lower dyadic endpoint, with absolute error at most tau=10^-9. This is not the full trajectory, all terminal components, a derivative, or a stationary-distribution sample.

## Space-time enclosure argument

For all real s, f(s) lies in (-1,1). Therefore B=[-1,1]^D is an invariant box for every allowed input. The unknown state at time N-K lies in B without computing the omitted prefix.

Select a patch of sites center-R,...,center+R. Initialize each patch coordinate to [-1,1] at time N-K. At every subsequent step, give each neighbor outside the patch the interval [-1,1] again. **Omitted neighbors are never set to zero and never silently ignored.** Use signed affine interval extrema followed by the increasing scalar function f.

**Induction:** assume the exact patch state lies in the current intervals. Every true exterior neighbor lies in [-1,1] by invariance. Signed affine extrema therefore enclose the true preactivation, and monotonicity of f encloses the next state. This establishes inclusion through time N for the original x0 and prescribed inputs. In fact it encloses every admissible initial state and every possible exterior history within B, which is stronger than needed for the specified zero initial state. No nearby shadowing orbit is substituted.

If the final center interval is [L/S,U/S] and U-L <= floor(S/10^9), reporting L/S has absolute error <=10^-9. Failure to pass this test produces abstention, not an approximate answer with an unsupported accuracy label.

**Finite arithmetic:** S=2^48. Endpoints L,U are signed integers. If n_lo,n_hi are the exact signed affine numerators formed from integer endpoints and b*S, the two next endpoints are

    floor[ S*n_lo / (16*S + |n_lo|) ],
    ceil [ S*n_hi / (16*S + |n_hi|) ].

The formula combines the affine denominator and nonlinear rational map without an intermediate floating operation. Signed division is corrected explicitly to mathematical floor/ceiling, including negative numerators. The executed C implementation uses signed __int128 products. Under its tested bounds p<=48, |a|<=64, input numerators <=1024, and states in [-S,S], products are far below the signed 128-bit limit. The output lower dyadic endpoint is also exactly representable in binary64. No libm transcendental accuracy assumption enters the certificate.

The adaptive schedule is deliberately simple: (K,R)=(8,1),(16,2),(32,4),(64,8),(128,16),(256,32), stopping at the first passing width or at the budget. Every failed attempt is included in cost. At K=128,R=16 the final rectangle has 4,224 cell updates and total attempted work is 24+80+288+1,088+4,224=5,704 interval cell updates. Work depends on the accepted rectangle; there is no guarantee that such a rectangle exists or stays small.

## Hostile test: reject apparent convergence that is not forgetting

With zero input and positive total gain 2, uniform states reduce to

    x_next = 2*x/(1+2*|x|).

The exact fixed states are -1/2,0,+1/2. Two positive initial states, 1/4 and 3/4, are separated by only 3.614007241618348e-20 after 64 steps, yet their value is about 1/2 while the requested zero-initial-state solution remains exactly 0. A two-trajectory convergence heuristic could therefore be wrong by 0.5. This follows from the exact positive-state solution x_n=1/[2+(1/x0-2)*2^-n].

The interval method correctly abstained. After its largest tested rectangle K=256,R=32, its width was 1.000000000000007 and it still enclosed both -1/2 and +1/2. Thus it did not equate two observed trajectories with all-state forgetting.

A separate scale probe requested center=500,000,000 at N=10^12 on a logical D=10^9 ring, G=32,signed coupling,A=32,seed=1. The finite patch admitted K=128,R=16, width 3.730349362740526e-11 and the same 5,704 updates. **No full trajectory/reference was computed for this large logical problem.** This is evidence from the local enclosure construction only, not a measured trillion-step speedup or an independently oracle-validated enormous simulation.

## What is promising, what remains limited, and the next falsifier

**Supported capability:** an auditable finite-arithmetic certificate can remove both the old history and distant sites from a selected terminal-state computation in this family. It can do so for positive and signed interactions and several gains above one, without a global all-input contraction theorem. Unlike the old chart line, success is not tied to an exponentially near-preperiodic Mandelbrot parameter. In admitted cases, most work is absent rather than merely evaluated faster.

**Hard boundaries:** bounded invariant state space; local interactions; a prescribed random-access input schedule; a sparse terminal query; and sufficient actual forgetting. Full trajectories have an output-size cost this experiment does not remove. State-dependent unknown inputs may require recovering the omitted past. Long-range coupling, weak drive, conserved quantities, persistent memory and interval wrapping may defeat this construction. A certificate for values is not a certificate for parameter sensitivities or gradients. The rational lattice is synthetic; no scientific application workload or industrial transfer is established. Known prior art prevents a claim that input forgetting or backward bounding is new.

**Decision:** preserve this as a new exploratory lineage with a real elimination capability, not as an invitation to tune the same toy's radius or chase another percent. The next discriminating task is one transfer test on a genuinely different bounded nonlinear model with correlated driving and a correctly matched short-memory/local comparator. Require a demonstrated new application capability or an informative obstruction. If the gain only survives deliberately strong, easily forgetting synthetic drive, report that boundary and redirect rather than extending this parameter sweep indefinitely. R-011 auditing remains a separate integrity backlog.
