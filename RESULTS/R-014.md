## Decision and falsifier

The omission construction transfers from R-013's rational recurrent network to a cubic, driven Allen-Cahn finite-difference time-stepper with slowly varying, spatially correlated forcing. It admitted 23/48 exploratory grid queries and three additional, fully replayed million-step queries. Relative to an implemented same-arithmetic temporal-only shortcut, local adaptive queries were 1.51x,5.46x,2.98x faster in those three cases. However, weak forcing has a provable phase-memory obstruction. The 1e-9 enclosure concerns the exact discrete recurrence, NOT the continuous equation: two time-discretization diagnostics differed from high-accuracy ODE integration by about 1e-4.

This is partial application transfer, not a validated PDE simulator, industrial gain or novel algorithm. The next useful capability is an end-to-end continuous-model error budget, not radius/amplitude tuning. Falsifiers were a missed fixed-IVP enclosure, no advantage against a short-memory rival, false admission of persistent phases, or confusion of omission error with discretization error. The 32-case pilot is disclosed; all 32 cases overlap the final 48-case grid. No pretest P/PA commit or retroactive frozen gate is claimed.

## Exact problem and correlated forcing

For D periodic sites with spacing one and h=1/64:

    u[n+1,i] = u[n,i] + h*(d*(u[n,i-1]-2*u[n,i]+u[n,i+1])
                            +u[n,i]-u[n,i]^3+b[n,i]),
    u[0,i] = (H(0,i,seed+1337) mod 17 - 8)/8.

The initial field is heterogeneous and exactly dyadic in [-1,1]. The output is ONE specified component u[N,center] of the exact rational recurrence, as a lower dyadic endpoint with absolute error <=1e-9. It is not a full field, trajectory, gradient, continuous-time state or spatial-continuum PDE solution.

Use d in {1,4}, physical amplitude A=amp/4 with amp in {1,2,4,8}, temporal knot spacing B in {256,1024}, spatial knot spacing W=16 and seeds {1,2,3}. Define q[t,j]=H(t,j,seed) mod 33 - 16. At n=tB+r, i=jW+s, bilinearly interpolate q[t,j],q[t,j+1],q[t+1,j],q[t+1,j+1] with weights r/B,s/W and multiply by amp/64. Spatial knot indices wrap modulo D/W. Hence |b|<=A<=2. This is piecewise-linear correlated forcing, not independently refreshed input; random access does not require a generator prefix.

H is explicitly the unsigned 64-bit hash used in R-013: z=seed XOR ((t+1)*0x9e3779b97f4a7c15) XOR ((j+1)*0xd1b54a32d192ed03); z=(z XOR (z>>30))*0xbf58476d1ce4e5b9; z=(z XOR (z>>27))*0x94d049bb133111eb; return z XOR (z>>31). Products and operands are reduced modulo 2^64.

Temporal knots are 4 or 16 physical time units apart. In disclosed 32768-sample traces, lag-one Pearson correlations were 0.99997558 and 0.99999878; lag-B/2 correlations were 0.70110 and 0.75150. These characterize those traces, not a population. The grid and forcing are not calibrated to a material or experiment.

## Enclosure derivation and arithmetic

On [-2,2]^D the complete Euler map has diagonal derivative

    1+(1-2*d-3*u_i^2)/64 >=45/64

and neighbor derivatives d/64>=0. It is therefore coordinatewise increasing. Evaluate the COMBINED monotone stencil at its lower and upper corners, rather than interval-evaluating u and -u^3 independently. The constant corners +/-2 map inward for |b|<=2, so [-2,2]^D is invariant. The row-sum derivative at the origin is 65/64>1; no global contraction guarantee is assumed.

Initialize included sites to [-2,2] at N-K. In a patch center-R through center+R, replace each omitted exterior neighbor by [-2,2] at EVERY step. The true initial patch state and every exterior history lie in those ranges. Monotonicity then proves inclusion at each subsequent step by induction for the original fixed initial condition. Exterior states are never silently zeroed or ignored.

With S=2^40 and integer endpoint values X,L,R,Bs=b*S:

    Q=(d*(L-2*X+R)+X+Bs)*S^2-X^3,
    lower_next=X+floor(Q/(64*S^2)),
    upper_next=X+ceil(Q/(64*S^2)).

Use lower neighbors for the lower update and upper neighbors for the upper update. Signed division is explicitly corrected to mathematical floor/ceiling. Tested input denominators divide S, so forcing interpolation is exact. Under state/input magnitudes <=2S and d<=4, |Q|<=44*S^3<2^127; signed __int128 intermediates fit. No floating arithmetic or transcendental assumption enters the interval kernel. Output dyadic endpoints are exactly representable in binary64.

Admit only when U-L<=floor(S/10^9); otherwise abstain. The fixed schedule is (K,R)=(256,4),(512,8),(1024,16),(2048,32),(4096,64),(8192,128). If a patch covers the ring, use the full periodic ring instead of duplicated sites. The temporal-only rival uses the same K schedule, selected-component gate and arithmetic, with all D sites at every attempt. Charge every failed attempt.

This is an inclusion argument plus an audited prototype, conditional on implementation correctness. It is not formal program verification, a PDE truncation-error certificate or a novelty result.

## Exact phase-memory obstruction

For ANY prescribed drive with |b[n,i]|<=3/8, both boxes

    P+=[1/2,2]^D,     P-=[-2,-1/2]^D

are invariant. At the constant +1/2 boundary, diffusion vanishes and u-u^3=3/8, so the most negative allowed drive cannot move outward. At +2 the reaction is -6 and points inward. The opposite corners give the negative box; coordinatewise monotonicity extends these checks to heterogeneous states.

Therefore an all-state omitted-history initialization spanning [-2,2]^D must retain queried width at least ONE at every horizon in this regime. Increasing history, radius or precision cannot establish all-state forgetting. The rational 3/8 threshold is sufficient, not claimed sharp.

A separate A=0.25,d=4,B=256,W=8,D=32,seed=1 test interval-replayed starts -1 and +1 for 16557 steps. Their queried states remained separated by at least 1.9931596370779516; the shortcut abstained. Zero forcing also abstained and retained exact fixed states +/-1. A=0.5 lies outside the theorem; its observed failures are not assigned that proof.

The original fixed IVP remains well posed. The obstruction concerns this broad all-state initialization, not every solver. Keeping a justified coarse phase label might help, but no phase-conditioned construction was tested.
