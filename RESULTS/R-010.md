**Date:** 2026-10-12 · **Parent:** [P-051](../PROMPTS/P-051.md) · **Track:** EXPLORATORY · **PREREGISTERED: NO** · **POD: NO** · **Evidence:** REPORTED · **PROOF:** NONE (exact-arithmetic conditional error derivation only) · **Novelty:** UNVERIFIED · **Gate:** original P-051 MPFR/speed gate NOT TESTED · **Opportunity:** CONDITIONAL; full parallel solve NOT ESTABLISHED.

## Mathematical construction and new test

Previously known Poincare/Koenigs linearization provides an exact repelling fixed-point chart. The exploratory synthesis here combines (1) exact fixed-z0 two-step anchoring, (2) independently evaluable chart interfaces, (3) a collapsed, nonsequential truncation-defect admission budget, and (4) direct continuation when the chart is no longer safe. **Neither linearization nor error propagation itself is claimed novel.** The tested question is whether this conditional structure avoids an expensive serial prefix *without* silently switching to a nearby shadowing orbit.

Use z0=0, z_(n+1)=z_n^2+c, c=-2+2^(-b), b in {128,256,384}, z2=c^2+c, alpha=(1+sqrt(1-4c))/2 and lambda=2 alpha>1. Define the degree-q truncated Poincare function

    phi_q(t)=sum_(k=1)^q a_k t^k; a1=1;
    a_k = [sum_(i=1)^(k-1) a_i a_(k-i)]/(lambda^k-lambda).

For each q, solve phi_q(t2)=z2-alpha by eight high-precision Newton iterations starting at t2=z2-alpha. Then set **w_n=alpha+phi_q(lambda^(n-2)t2)** for any requested n>=2. This computes the candidate without forming z3,...,z_(n-1). The first two states are anchored exactly, not fitted.

**Conditional truncation-defect budget.** The functional identity phi(lambda t)=lambda phi(t)+phi(t)^2 yields, for truncated phi_q,

    r_j := w_(j+1)-w_j^2-c
         = -sum_(k=q+1)^(2q) d_k (t2 lambda^(j-2))^k,
    d_k = sum_(max(1,k-q)<=i<=min(q,k-1)) a_i a_(k-i) >= 0.

For e_j=z_j-w_j, the exact identity is e_(j+1)=(2w_j+e_j)e_j-r_j. Fix a candidate n and tolerance eta=1e-7. Define T=|t2|lambda^(n-2), M=|alpha|+sum_(k=1)^q |a_k|T^k, B=2M+eta, and E2=|w2-z2|. Since |w_j|<=M for 2<=j<=n, a bootstrap with |e_j|<=eta gives

    H_n = B^(n-2) E2
       + sum_(k=q+1)^(2q) d_k |t2|^k
         (lambda^(k(n-2))-B^(n-2))/(lambda^k-B).

When lambda^k=B use the geometric-sum limit. With exact (or rigorously enclosed) input, coefficient and chart arithmetic, **H_n<=eta implies |z_j-w_j|<=eta for every 2<=j<=n**, by positive-term induction. No serial orbit prefix is needed to evaluate H_n. This is a derived conditional bound, *not* a validated numerical certificate: the experimental alpha, lambda, coefficients, t2 and H_n were evaluated with non-interval floating arithmetic, so numerical roundoff was not formally enclosed.

## Actual computation

Python 3 + mpmath at **560 decimal digits** (mp.mp.dps = 560), deterministic b in {128,256,384}, q in {8,16,32}, n=b/2+{2,4,6}. Construct coefficients by the displayed triangular recurrence, solve t2 with eight Newton updates, evaluate w_n by Horner and exponentiation, and compute H_n from the displayed formula. The *independent-algorithm* accuracy oracle computes the same exact z0 IVP by serial high-precision recurrence, not by chart prediction. **Segment interface correction probe:** independently chart-seed interface nodes from n=2 through 132 at spacings L=8 or L=32 (short final segment), evaluate each F_L and derivative at its seeded state, compute r_j=F_L(b_j)-b_(j+1), and one linearized shooting correction delta0=0, delta_(j+1)=r_j+F'_L(b_j)delta_j. For b=256, q=32, the maximum initial residual for both spacings was 2.259608e-39; after correction the maximum residual and last-endpoint discrepancy were 6.84879e-157. **Correction was actually computed by a serial prefix, not timed as a parallel scan.** A catastrophically wrong *last* interface can also be trivially replaced from an earlier good segment: this is not evidence of global Newton convergence from arbitrary seeds. No parallel-speed claim follows.

## What the evidence establishes and does not

- **Supported conditional observation:** a high-precision seed at step 132 was generated from exact step 2 without calculating the intervening serial orbit; its ideal truncation budget correctly distinguished several safe and failing chart evaluations. A chart-to-direct switch avoided a dramatic error at step 134.
- **Failure/boundary:** high-degree direct chart evaluation is not unconditionally safe after exit from the repelling neighborhood. Long post-handoff work remains sequential and may erase any benefit. The original P-051 256–4096-step segmented MPFR gate remains **UNTESTED**.
- **Arithmetic issue:** an initial 260-digit exploratory pass for b=384 hit roundoff near 1e-150 at an early q=32 interface while ideal truncation H was near 1e-198. Rerunning at 560 digits corrected the displayed truncation values but still gives **no interval certificate**; numeric H alone cannot be reported as rigorous certification.
- **No priority claim:** the chart is classical, the elementary defect bound is derived here but may have earlier analogues, and broad-domain usefulness, total high-precision cost, originality and independent reproduction are UNVERIFIED. No generated script, raw data, or dependency was committed to this documentation-only branch.
