## Capability tested and exact model

The task is one selected terminal-state component of a bounded driven nonlinear ring, not the full trajectory:

    x(0,i) = 0,
    x(t+1,i) = f((a0*x(t,i) + al*x(t,i-1) + ar*x(t,i+1) + b(t,i))/16),
    f(s) = s/(1+abs(s)).

Indices wrap around a ring of D sites. For integer G in {12,20,24,32,48,64}, a0=G/2, al=G/4, ar=+G/4 or -G/4. The global infinity-norm Lipschitz bound is G/16. Input b(t,i) is a prescribed integer in [-A,A], with A in {2,8,16,32,64}, generated by the exact unsigned 64-bit hash in `replay.c`. Both C and independently written Python generation were checked. Input generation is random-access: no input-generator history has to be replayed. Main-sweep seeds are 1 through 5.

**Construction:** begin a recent patch at time N-K with every coordinate in [-1,1]. At every subsequent time, replace each omitted exterior neighbor by [-1,1] again. Propagate signed affine interval extrema and the increasing scalar function f. Expand K and spatial radius R together until the center interval width is <=10^-9 or the fixed budget is exhausted.

**Inclusion proof:** f maps every real argument into (-1,1), so the original state at N-K and all omitted boundary histories lie in the chosen invariant ranges. If the patch intervals contain the exact state at one step, signed affine extrema contain every exact preactivation, and monotonicity of f encloses the next state. Induction gives an enclosure for the original zero-initial-state query without computing its omitted prefix or exterior trajectory. It also encloses other admissible initial states; no nearby shadowing solution is substituted.

With S=2^48 and exact affine integer numerators n_lo,n_hi, the next endpoints are

    floor(S*n_lo/(16*S+abs(n_lo))),
    ceil(S*n_hi/(16*S+abs(n_hi))).

Signed division is corrected to mathematical floor/ceiling. The tested coefficients, input bounds and p<=48 keep signed 128-bit intermediates safely within range. The implementation contains no transcendental floating evaluation in the enclosure. Report the lower dyadic endpoint only when U-L <= floor(S/10^9). Rejection is abstention, not an accuracy-qualified answer.

The fixed exploration schedule is (K,R)=(8,1),(16,2),(32,4),(64,8),(128,16),(256,32). All failed attempts count toward cost. The 128-step, 33-site accepted patch entails 4,224 final interval cell updates, or 5,704 including earlier attempts. For an admitted query the work depends on this local rectangle, not directly on the entire N by D history. There is no guarantee that a small admissible rectangle exists.

## Failure that matters

For zero forcing and positive total gain 2, uniform states obey x_next=2*x/(1+2*abs(x)). Exact fixed states are -1/2,0,+1/2. Starts at 1/4 and 3/4 are separated by only 3.614007241618348e-20 after 64 steps, yet their common-looking value is wrong by about 0.5 for the requested zero initial condition. The exact positive-state formula is x_n=1/[2+(1/x0-2)*2^-n]. Agreement of two probes therefore does not establish forgetting of all admissible histories.

The interval method correctly abstained: even at K=256,R=32 its width was 1.000000000000007 and it included both nonzero fixed states. This check was executed again. The bundle also contains a huge logical-domain local certificate, but NO full oracle or speedup claim for that huge case.

## Novelty, transfer and stopping boundary

Input-dependent forgetting, pullback images, coupling from the past and bounding histories are established ingredients. See [Manjunath and Jaeger, Echo State Property Linked to an Input](https://www.ai.rug.nl/minds/uploads/ESP_NC.pdf) and [Propp and Wilson, Exact Sampling with Coupled Markov Chains](https://mathexp2018.metelu.net/books/ProppWilson-1996-ExactSamplingWithCoupledMarkovChainsAndApplicationsToStatisticalMechanics.pdf); the fuller source/search log is in R-013. Their stochastic or asymptotic theorems are not imported unchanged as guarantees for this deterministic finite-time model.

The tested assembly is promising but not proven original: finite-arithmetic selected-component omission with signed coupling and unknown exterior histories. It needs a bounded invariant set, local interactions, accessible prescribed inputs, sparse terminal outputs and sufficient actual forgetting. It does not reconstruct a full trajectory, certify gradients or remove conserved modes. Strong synthetic forcing makes forgetting easier; no scientific-model transfer has yet been demonstrated.

**Next falsifier: transfer, not tuning.** Test one genuinely different bounded nonlinear model under correlated driving with an appropriate short-memory/local comparator. Seek a new application capability or a mathematical obstruction. Do not default to another radius/gain sweep. The original P-051 frozen gate and R-011 audit remain separate, unchanged work.
