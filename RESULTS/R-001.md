# R-001 · Polynomial flow map versus numerical integration

**Date:** 2026-10-12 · **Parent:** [P-010](../PROMPTS/P-010.md) · **Evidence:** REPORTED, not frozen/reproduced · **Decision:** KILL original speed claim.

**Goal and gate:** Replace repeatedly integrating a smooth, periodically forced nonlinear oscillator with one fitted period polynomial, maintaining worst sampled state error ≤1e-7 and at least 3× cold gain versus an optimized rival.

**Initial comparison:** 100,000 evaluations: SciPy batched integration ~5.59 s; degree-six fitted polynomial evaluation ~0.145 s (38.6× warm). Approximate build ~1.21 s; previously reported cold ratio ~4.12×. Independent 256-sample validation maximum ~7.18e-8.

**Stronger opponent:** JIT-compiled RK4, checked against accurate integration at the stated target. At 100,000 cases, compiled RK4 ~0.838 s, polynomial approach **~1.306 s including build**: polynomial **1.56× slower cold**.

**Conclusion:** Warm acceleration against interpreter-heavy baseline was misleading. No new industrial win.

**Provenance gaps:** No committed script, dependency lock, exact input distribution, repeated timing range, or raw error table. Earlier claim is retained but **superseded**. These numbers must not be called independently reproduced.
