# R-004 · Direct composition overturns the Koenigs result

**Date:** 2026-10-12 · **Parent:** [P-022](../PROMPTS/P-022.md) · **Evidence:** REPORTED · **Decision:** KILL preferred Koenigs operator on this toy.

**Fairness upgrade:** Instead of evaluating a degree-eight one-period polynomial 64 times, directly compose it to obtain a local 64-period polynomial. Compare both cold build and evaluation with the Koenigs route on the same initial states.

**Predeclared gate:** Maximum empirical state error ≤1e-7. If polynomial composition erases the apparent ≥3× Koenigs speed-up, reject Koenigs preference.

**Reported 8192-state cold timings:** Direct polynomial composition+evaluation **~0.001265 s**; Koenigs build+evaluation **~0.006672 s**. Direct composition is **~5.27× faster than Koenigs** in this toy test. Composition-vs-repeated-map worst sampled difference ~1.59e-11.

**What it does NOT prove:** No industrial solver was benchmarked; no validated domain bound; no repelling-cycle case; no new technique invented. The stronger result overturns the apparent gain in R-003, not the mathematical usefulness of Koenigs generally.
