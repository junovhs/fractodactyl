## Interpretation and handoff

**KILL degree-five Taylor kick as a claimed ≥3× shortcut on this sampled-data system.** Direct exact affine propagation is already highly optimized; a polynomial trades generality for only a ~2× warmed gain and essentially **no cold gain**. P-030's *continuous state-triggered event* problem remains untested: this model's switching time is sampled at period boundaries, not discovered by root finding. The cheapest next experiment is to test a continuous threshold-crossing (event-time) two-state model with a guard-sensitive map and compare it with optimized exact-affine root solve/event integration, including crossing conditioning and itinerary classes. This is a changed mechanism, not a higher-degree retry.
