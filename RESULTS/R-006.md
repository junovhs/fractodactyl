## Mathematical model and independent oracle

Dimensionless sampled-data PWM surrogate with `x=(i,v)` and `x'=A x+b_mode`, `A=[[0,-1],[1,-0.5]]`, `b_on=(1,0)`, `b_off=(0,0)`, period `T=0.3`. The controller reads `v` **at each period start** and computes `d=clip(0.5+0.6*(0.5-v)+p,0.2,0.8)`; ON for `Td`, OFF for `T(1-d)`. Saturation classes: `d_raw<0.2`, `0.2≤d_raw≤0.8`, `d_raw>0.8`. This has one **scheduled** switch per period, not a continuous state-triggered event.

Exact map: `x_next=E(T)x+A^{-1}(E(T)-E(T*(1-d)))b_on`, `E(t)=exp(A*t)`. Candidate approximates only the kick `C(d)` by its **order-4 Taylor series about d=0.5**. For `k≥1`, coefficient is `(-1)^(k+1)*T^k/k! * A^(k-1)*E(T/2)*b_on`. Both evaluators clip the duty exactly.

**Independent oracle:** for every ON segment, `scipy.linalg.expm([[A,b_on],[0,0]]*T*d)` on the augmented affine system, then `scipy.linalg.expm(A*T*(1-d))` for OFF. No reliance on the candidate coefficient recurrence. Reference and candidate each recalculate duty from **their own evolving state**.

Inputs generated by `numpy.random.default_rng(20261012)`: sequential independent uniform draws of **128 currents [0.15,0.35]**, **128 voltages [0.35,0.65]**, then **128 parameters [-0.35,0.35]**. 32 complete periods, 4,096 regime decisions. First three `(i,v,p)`: `(.2721161187383,.5587300373135,-.1089085425999)`, `(.2325039481207,.4599137041919,-.0236667833960)`, `(.3283832751398,.5122009569977,.2733093882062)`.
