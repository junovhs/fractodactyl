# P-020 · Koenigs jump on a driven system

**Status:** KILLED BROAD DOMAIN · Parent: P-010 · Result: [R-002](../RESULTS/R-002.md)

**Question:** Can a one-dimensional Koenigs coordinate jump across many forcing periods of a nonlinear oscillator accurately enough to replace repeated map evaluation?

**Mechanism:** Transform a fitted Poincaré return near a hyperbolic fixed point into approximately linear dynamics; invert after jumping.

**Fair opponent:** The already-fitted period map, iterated or composed with equal preparation allowance.

**Test:** Build eighth-order charts and check independently integrated trajectories before benchmarking.

**Kill:** Reject at max absolute state error above 1e-7.

**Outcome:** The broad chart failed dramatically at the first accuracy gate.

**Follow-up:** P-021 narrows the chart and studies amortization.
