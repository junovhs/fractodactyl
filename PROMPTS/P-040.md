# P-040 · Select the cheapest safe operator

**Status:** UNTESTED · REOPENED FOR CONSTRUCTIVE EXTENSIONS (2026-10-12 policy correction) · Parent: R-004 · Priority 2

**Scope note:** The 3× gate is P-040-specific, not a rejection rule for smaller accurate gains. Register any changed economic test separately.

**Question:** Can an automatic chooser beat a fixed operator by selecting numerical integration, direct polynomial composition, or Koenigs per domain and horizon?

**Mechanism:** Estimate costs and propagated approximation bounds before selecting the operator; allow abstention.

**Fair opponent:** An oracle-selected conventional method with identical reusable data, charged for its setup.

**Probe:** Sweep radii, horizons and batch sizes on the frozen scalar oscillator; score end-to-end time and worst validation error.

**Kill:** Max state error above 1e-7; or total cold speed gain under 3× relative to a realistic same-math baseline.

**Risk:** Operator-choice overhead may exceed savings. A win relative to an artificially weak fixed method is not enough.
