**Closest:** Zhang et al. (2008), Guglielmi/Hairer (2022); event location is established.
**Question:** Can quadratic event-time prediction plus two safeguarded Newton corrections reduce return-map cost?
**Model:** `x'=-0.8x+c`, `y'=0.55x-0.6y+d`; ON `(c,d)=(0.2,1.4)`, OFF `(-0.1,-0.8)`. Each `T=0.8` period begins ON, switches once at the first upward crossing of `y=0.25+p`, and then remains OFF.
**Candidate:** Exact mode flow; quadratic predictor, two Newton steps, fallback to exact root if residual >1e-12 or unsafe.
**Rivals:** Numba-compiled exact-flow safeguarded Newton and bracketed bisection, both cached.
**Probe:** 128 trajectories ×16 periods, seed 20261030; independent oracle. Timing: 128×16×128 updates, seed 20261031; three repeats.
**Gate:** State error ≤1e-7; event-time error ≤1e-9; zero itinerary mistakes. Measure cold/warm and savings; no speed floor.
