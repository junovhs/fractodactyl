# P-070 · Multistate event-domain generalization

**Status:** UNTESTED · Parent: P-030 · Blocked on P-030

**Scope note:** This historical 3× threshold is not a general requirement; retain credible smaller gains under [GOAL](../GOAL.md).

**Question:** If automatic switching-map admission succeeds in a two-state model, does it survive a coupled, higher-dimensional system with multiple switching surfaces?

**Mechanism:** State-and-parameter Taylor jets on event-itinerary cells, fallback on uncertain crossings, explicit propagated remainder bounds.

**Fair opponent:** Compiled event-aware integration, periodic shooting, direct cached-map composition.

**Probe:** Minimal three-state switched oscillator or converter, 100 independent parameter sets and 32 periods.

**Kill:** Wrong event branches, >1e-7 worst state error, unusably small certified domains, or cold gain under 3×.

**Decision rule:** Do not run until P-030 passes a cheaper proof-of-mechanism experiment.
