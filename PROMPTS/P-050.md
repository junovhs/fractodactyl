# P-050 · Whole-orbit Newton prefix scan

**Status:** KILLED GLOBAL · Parent: P-000 · Result: [R-005](../RESULTS/R-005.md)

**Question:** Can a million-step, million-bit Mandelbrot reference orbit be solved via whole-orbit Newton corrections using parallel affine prefix scans and logarithmically many precision-doubling sweeps?

**Mechanism:** Linearize the entire orbit and scan the bidiagonal correction recurrence.

**Fair opponent:** Sequential optimized MPFR reference iteration, charging every high-precision sweep and guard bit.

**Probe:** Small bounded but strongly amplifying orbit; MPFR arithmetic costs from 1k to 100k bits.

**Kill:** A non-small initial correction, increasing residual, or over 4× total work versus sequential.

**Outcome:** Double-seeded global Newton diverged; full-mantissa sweep arithmetic exceeded the threshold at high precision.

**Follow-up:** P-051.
