# P-021 · Shrink domain, increase batch

**Status:** CONDITIONAL / SUPERSEDED · Parent: R-002 · Result: [R-003](../RESULTS/R-003.md)

**Gate note:** The 3× threshold belonged to this historical test; missing it did not make accurate smaller improvements worthless. Its fairer opponent is [R-004](../RESULTS/R-004.md).

**Question:** If a Koenigs map fails on a large domain, does adaptive shrinking yield enough accurate coverage and reuse to justify it?

**Mechanism:** Restrict initial states to a smaller neighborhood and reuse the computed jump across a large batch.

**Fair opponent:** Repeated evaluation of the fitted return map; then direct composition of that same map.

**Test:** Compare 512 and 8,192 initial states over 64 returns; count construction cost.

**Kill:** Max state error above 1e-7, or cold gain below 3× against the strongest same-math rival.

**Outcome:** A local win against repeated evaluation was later overturned.

**Follow-up:** P-022.
