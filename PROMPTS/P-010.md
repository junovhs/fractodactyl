# P-010 · Fit a reusable one-period map

**Status:** SUPERSEDED · Parent: P-000 · Result: [R-001](../RESULTS/R-001.md)

**Question:** Does a compact polynomial for the one-period flow of a forced nonlinear oscillator beat repeated accurate integration?

**Mechanism:** Fit a reusable state/forcing-dependent return polynomial from high-accuracy integration.

**Fair opponent:** Compiled and vectorized numerical integration, not interpreter-bound SciPy alone.

**Test:** 100,000 initial states; compare map construction plus evaluation to compiled RK4 at matched independent reference accuracy.

**Kill:** If the map loses cold, or exceeds 1e-7 maximum absolute state error, reject the speed claim.

**Outcome:** Initial warm-looking win was overturned by a compiled RK4 rival.
