# P-022 · Direct composition versus Koenigs

**Status:** CLOSED · Parent: R-003 · Result: [R-004](../RESULTS/R-004.md)

**Question:** Is Koenigs linearization actually the cheapest way to jump 64 periods when we already have a compact degree-eight period map?

**Mechanism:** Compose the same return polynomial with itself, truncating to controlled degree and testing its local error.

**Fair opponent:** Koenigs chart build, inversion and jump with equally counted setup.

**Test:** 8,192 states, 64 periods; measure cold timings and errors against repeated maps / independent integration.

**Kill:** If composition is accurate and erases the Koenigs advantage, reject Koenigs preference.

**Outcome:** Composition was about 5.27× faster cold in this toy experiment. Not a novel industrial win.

**Branches:** P-030, P-040, P-060.
